# 🌱 Module 2: NDVI Dashboard

NDVI (Normalized Difference Vegetation Index) measures vegetation health.

Use colors like:

🟤 Bare soil

🟡 Poor vegetation

🟢 Healthy vegetation

Dark Green = Very healthy crops

Farmers can see:

* crop stress
* drought
* vegetation changes
* forest loss

Charts could include:

```
Average NDVI

Weekly Trend

Monthly Trend

Yearly Trend
```

For example, Agriculture could eventually show farmland boundaries, crop-health indicators, and moisture trends for a selected area. Those require additional datasets and analysis; selecting the Agriculture button alone won't produce them.
